## 系统里短标题 / 短摘要（字数限制）

**Title（≤16 words）**
```text
Information value of gauges and QPF for Potomac-James streamflow forecasts
```

**Abstract（≤150 words）**
```text
Whether upstream gauges improve downstream forecasts is well known; the open question is how skill is partitioned among routing memory, machine learning, and precipitation foresight, and whether that partition transfers across basins. We compare Potomac and James River outlets using USGS and Open-Meteo data, lagged-upstream routing baselines, ablation, P90 flood CSI, and a 2024 GFS QPF ladder versus an oracle ceiling. Next-day skill is routing-dominated with basin-dependent upstream gains; 3-7-day observation-only forecasts are information-limited; raw GFS QPF helps at 1 day but can degrade uncorrected longer leads. Results guide mid-Atlantic gauge and QPF design.
```
